# Scout Game backtest — November 2026

Generated by `node scripts/backtest-scout-game.mjs` on 2026-11-09. 115 finished regular-season rounds across 7 leagues, 41,668 player-fixture rows. Supabase reads only; no API-Football requests.

## Findings

1. **The agreed ladder (tier 1 → 5×) breaks the game.** Picking the five top-tier players scores 19.7 a round; five random tiered players score 37.0 and the five bottom-tier players 37.8. Scouting the best players is the worst strategy.
2. **Why: tiers predict points, but not by 5×.** A tier-1 pick earns 0.58 of what a tier-5 pick earns (section 2), so any tier-1 multiplier above about 1.73× makes the bottom tier out-earn the top. With no multiplier at all the order is right (top 19.7 > random 15.4 > bottom 9.2).
3. **Recommended ladder: half compensation, 1 · 1.05 · 1.1 · 1.2 · 1.35, unranked 1.35×.** Each tier is paid back half its earning gap. Top 19.7 beats random 16.9 (+17%); bottom 11.7 stays within reach (0.69 of random); unknowns alone score 8.2 and never win. A tier-1 player who delivers still pays 35% more than a star with the same line.
4. **Captain: additive, as agreed.** Additive and multiplicative are within a point of each other under the recommended ladder; additive is simpler to explain. The **6× cap stays in the rules**, but under the recommended ladder no pick can reach it (the maximum is 1.35 + 1 captain + 0.5 contrarian = 2.85×).
5. **Exception: Argentina.** Top tiers trail random there (12.6 vs 15.6) under every ladder, flat included: the composite predicts Argentine points poorly. Worth revisiting the composite weights for that league, not the ladder.
6. **A typical fan round** (five Série A regulars from one club) scores a median of 16.3 under the recommended rules (p10 6.3, p90 33.8), against 33.0 under the agreed ones.

## Rules under test

- **Base points**: the agreed table in `api/_lib/scout-game.js` (the live scoring code, imported, not copied).
- **Tier**: from `player_tiers_at(league, season, deadline)`: each round is tiered only on fixtures that kicked off before its deadline.
- **Multiplier (default)**: tier 5 → 1×, 4 → 2×, 3 → 3×, 2 → 4×, 1 → 5×; unranked (below the minutes threshold) → 3×.
- **Captain (default, additive)**: +100% of base, i.e. +1 on the pick's multiplier. Compared against the multiplicative version (×2 on the whole multiplier).
- **Cap**: no single pick's effective multiplier exceeds **6×**, in either captain mode.
- **Negative base**: counted once, unscaled.
- **Contrarian bonus**: not simulated (it needs real ownership; there are no real entries yet).

## Method

- **Pool** for a round: players with at least one appearance in that competition before the deadline whose latest club plays in the round. "Tiered" means at or above the minutes threshold (max(270, 30% of club minutes)).
- **A pick who does not play scores 0**: once a fixture is ingested, no row means did not play. Strategies pay for picking a star who is injured or rested.
- **Captain** = the pick with the highest expected value: mean base points per appearance before the deadline × tier multiplier.
- **At most one goalkeeper** per entry, as in the game.
- **(a)** the five highest tiers (composite breaks ties); **(b)** five random tiered players, 200 draws per round; **(c)** the five lowest tiers; **(d)** five random *unranked* players, 200 draws per round: the "unknowns alone" test.
- Random draws use a fixed seed (20261109), so the report reproduces exactly.
- **Early rounds**, where fewer than 50 players in the round are tiered yet, are reported separately: tiers are thin there and every strategy collapses toward "unranked". 21 of 115 rounds are early.
