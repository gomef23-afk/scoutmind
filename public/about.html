How it works
Follow Your Club,
Everywhere It Plays
Free, no subscription, no locked leagues. Pick your clubs once and the whole app arranges itself around them.
01
Pick your clubs
Choose from 146 clubs across seven leagues: Série A, the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, the Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and Argentina's Liga Profesional. One main club, as many others as you like.
02
Get their news
Around 500 stories a day from ten outlets in six languages, tagged to the clubs they are actually about. Read in the languages you choose — and anything about your clubs reaches you whatever language it is written in.
03
Follow match day
Fixtures and results grouped by date in your own timezone, with your clubs first: the last result and the next match, always labelled as such. Live scores update while games are on.
04
See the real numbers
League position, points, goals scored and conceded per game, and pass accuracy — measured from this season's matches, not estimated. Where we do not have a number, we show nothing rather than guess.
05
Join the community
Every club has its own page: polls, a transfer wishlist, and insights from analysts with a verified badge. Free to read, free to post.
06
Dig into the squad
Scout Mode compares a squad against its league average and suggests the player profiles that would strengthen it. One feature among the rest, and still running on a fixed sample of players — it says so at the top of the page.

FAQ
Common Questions
Where does the data come from?
Fixtures, results, league tables and season statistics come from API-Football. News headlines come from the public RSS feeds of ten outlets — ge, ESPN Brasil, BBC Sport, Sky Sports, Olé, AS, Marca, La Gazzetta dello Sport, kicker and RMC Sport. We store a headline, a short summary and a link, and every story links back to the publisher.
Which leagues do you cover?
Seven, with 146 clubs: Brazil's Série A, the Premier League, La Liga, Italy's Serie A, the Bundesliga, Ligue 1 and Argentina's Liga Profesional. MLS is next. If a club is not in one of those, you will not find it yet — we would rather cover fewer leagues properly than list a lot of them with nothing behind it.
Is ScoutMind really free?
Yes. Every feature is free for every user — no subscriptions, no trials, no locked leagues, no paid tier planned. You need an account only to pick your clubs, post and vote; reading works signed out.
What is the community?
Every club has its own page where supporters run polls, vote on a transfer wishlist, and read insights from analysts with a verified badge. Journalists, licensed scouts and agents can apply for that badge for free.
What about the scouting tools?
Scout Mode compares a squad against its league average and suggests player profiles to strengthen. It still runs on a fixed sample of players rather than live data, and it says so at the top of the page. It moves onto real player data alongside the rest of the app.
Which languages does the feed speak?
Six: Portuguese, English, Spanish, Italian, German and French. You choose which ones your feed shows, and anything about a club you follow reaches you whatever language it was written in.
