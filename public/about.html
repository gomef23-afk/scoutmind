How it works
Follow Your Club,
Everywhere It Plays
Free, no subscription, no locked leagues. Pick your clubs once and the whole app arranges itself around them.
01
Pick your clubs
Choose from every club in the leagues we cover. One main club, as many others as you like.
02
Get their news
Around 500 stories a day from established outlets, tagged to the clubs they are actually about. Read in the languages you choose — and anything about your clubs reaches you whatever language it is written in.
03
Follow match day
Fixtures and results grouped by date in your own timezone, with your clubs first: the last result and the next match, always labelled as such. Live scores update while games are on.
04
See the real numbers
League position, points, goals scored and conceded per game, and pass accuracy — measured from this season's matches, not estimated. Where we do not have a number, we show nothing rather than guess.
05
Join the community
Every club has its own page: polls, a transfer wishlist, and insights from analysts with a verified badge. Free to read, free to post.
06
Play the Scout Game
Each round, pick five players and name a captain who earns extra. Points come from real match stats: what your players actually do on the pitch. Start a private league and play against your friends. Free, just for fun.

FAQ
Common Questions
Where does the data come from?
Fixtures, results, league tables and season statistics come from API-Football. News headlines come from the public RSS feeds of established outlets. We store a headline, a short summary and a link, and every story links back to the publisher.
Which leagues do you cover?
The leagues listed above. Those in the Scout Game are playable in the game; the others are covered (matches, live scores, tables, club pages and rooms) but not playable. If a club is not in one of them, you will not find it yet.
Is ScoutMind really free?
Yes. Every feature is free for every user — no subscriptions, no trials, no locked leagues, no paid tier planned. You need an account only to pick your clubs, post and vote; reading works signed out.
What is the community?
Every club has its own page where supporters run polls, vote on a transfer wishlist, and read insights from analysts with a verified badge. Journalists, licensed scouts and agents can apply for that badge for free.
What is the Scout Game?
A free game that runs alongside the season. Before each round's deadline you pick five players from the leagues in the game and name one of them captain. When the matches are played, each player scores from real match stats, and your captain earns extra. Rankings show how you compare with everyone, and private leagues let you play against a group of friends with a join link. No prizes, just fun.
Which languages does the feed speak?
Portuguese (from Brazil and Portugal), English, Spanish, Italian, German, French, Dutch and Turkish. You choose which ones your feed shows, and anything about a club you follow reaches you whatever language it was written in.
