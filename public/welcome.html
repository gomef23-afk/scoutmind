The Scout Game

  1. Pick five and a captain

    Before each round's deadline, choose five players from the competitions we cover and name one captain.

  2. Real match stats score

    Goals, assists, saves and the rest, from the real matches. Your captain earns extra.

  3. Private leagues with friends

    Start a league, share the join link, and see who reads the game best each round.

What you get

  • Your club's Home

    Its table position, next match and the open Scout Game round at a glance.

  • Club rooms

    One official room per club, for the people who follow it.

  • News with reactions

    Headlines from the outlets that cover your club, with a link to every story.

  • Tables and matches

    League tables, fixtures and results in your own time zone.
